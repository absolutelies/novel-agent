## Rule 1 (R1): "Not A, But B" Pattern Removal

### English Patterns to Scan
- "not A, but B"
- "not A, is B"
- "not only A, but also B"
- "rather than A, it's B"
- "more than just A, B"

### Processing — 99% Rewrite

Default: Rewrite all. Keep only if BOTH conditions met:
1. Removal causes plot logic break
2. Contrast contains reader-can't-deduce information

### English Rewrite Methods

1. **Single affirmation**: "not brave, but desperate" → "that was desperation"
2. **Dual affirmation**: "not cold, but controlled" → "controlled, and self-protective"
3. **Concrete description**: "not fear, but awe" → "his knees bent without his willing"
4. **Keep B directly**: "not wind, but crying" → "that was crying"

## Rule 2 (R2): Sparse Formatting Removal

### English Patterns to Scan
- Non-dialogue lines ≤15 characters, alone
- 2+ consecutive short sentences on separate lines
- Paragraphs with 1-2 sentences (not scene transitions)

### Processing
- Merge into preceding paragraph
- Rewrite as complete sentence

### English Exceptions
- Dialogue lines (with quotation marks)
- Chapter titles, scene dividers (`***`, `---`)
- Single exclamation/sound effects ("Bang.", "Snap—")
- Deliberate literary breaks

---

## English Dialogue Format Check

Verify dialogue uses Western format:
- Double quotes: "text" (NOT 「text」)
- Punctuation inside: "text," not "text",
- New paragraph for each speaker
