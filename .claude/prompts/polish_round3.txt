## Rule 6 (R6): English Sentence Rhythm

### Scan Targets

1. **Structure Repetition**: 3+ same English structure
   - S-V-O × 3 → change one
   - "He [verb] the [noun]" × 3 → restructure one

2. **Length Repetition**: 3+ within ±5 words
   - 15, 16, 14 word sentences → vary one

3. **Ending Repetition**: Same paragraph endings
   - "..., and [result]" × 3 → vary one

### English Processing
Change ONE sentence in pattern.

### English Exceptions
- Dialogue patterns (character speech)
- Deliberate parallelism
- Action rhythm

---

## Rule 7 (R7): English Three-Part Overuse

### Detection
English AI tendency for triads:
- "A, B, and C" × multiple
- Three parallel short sentences grouped repeatedly

### English Processing
Break to 2 or 4 parts.

### English Examples
- "strong, brave, and loyal" → "strong and brave—loyal above all"
- "laughed. cried. screamed." → "laughed, then cried. When she screamed..."

### Keep when
- Thematic three intentional
- Rhetorical emphasis (one instance)
- Character triad speech pattern
