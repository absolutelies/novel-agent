## Round 1: Sentence Patterns (R1-R2)

**Input**: Original chapter text

**Apply**:
- R1: "Not A but B" → single affirmation
- R2: Sparse formatting → merge paragraphs

**Output**: Pattern-cleaned text → Pass to Round 2

---

## Round 2: AI Vocabulary (R3-R5, R9-R10)

**Input**: Round 1 output

**Apply**:
- R3: Connector words → delete (Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally)
- R4: Synonym rotation → unify
- R5: Filler phrases → delete
- R9: Delve family → replace (delve, tapestry, testament, symphony, realm, kaleidoscope, nuance, myriad, intricate, ethereal, palpable, profound)
- R10: Academic vocabulary → replace (implement, facilitate, demonstrate, crucial, significant, essential)

**Output**: Vocabulary-cleaned text → Pass to Round 3

---

## Round 3: Rhythm & Structure (R6-R7)

**Input**: Round 2 output

**Apply**:
- R6: Structure repetition → vary (same S-V-O × 3, same length ±5 words × 3)
- R7: Three-part patterns → 2 or 4 parts

**Output**: Rhythm-varied text → Pass to Round 4

---

## Round 4: Fiction Cliches (R11-R12)

**Input**: Round 3 output

**Apply**:
- R11: Body/emotion cliches → delete/rewrite (shivers ran down, heart pounded, breath caught, wave of emotion, couldn't help but)
- R12: Description metaphors → delete (like a symphony, like a tapestry, wind whispered, sunlight danced)

**Output**: Cliche-free text → Pass to Round 5

---

## Round 5: Dialogue Humanization (R13-R16)

**Input**: Round 4 output

**Apply**:
- R13: Perfect → natural dialogue (fragments, interruptions, fillers)
- R14: Direct → subtext (emotion statements → actions)
- R15: Power dynamics (speech length variation)
- R16: Conversational imperfections (trail offs, restarts)

**Output**: Human dialogue text → Pass to Round 6

---

## Round 6: Detail Pruning (R8, R16-extended)

**Input**: Round 5 output

**Apply**:
- R8: Useless details → delete (with protection for action scenes, intimate scenes, key dialogue, character appearance)
- R16-extended: Redundant reactions → keep strongest only

**Output**: Pruned text → Pass to Round 7

---

## Round 7: Burstiness Injection (R17-R20)

**Input**: Round 6 output

**Apply**:
- R17: Sentence length variation (burst patterns by scene type)
- R18: Paragraph variation (mix 1-sentence, 5-sentence, 10-sentence)
- R19: Opening word variation (avoid same opener × 3+)
- R20: Ending variation (avoid same ending structure × 3+)

**Output**: Bursty text → Pass to Round 8

---

## Round 8: Final Human Pass (R21-R25)

**Input**: Round 7 output

**Apply**:
- R21: Personal voice (opinions, attitude, personality)
- R22: Imperfection injection (fragments, run-ons, And/But starts)
- R23: Specificity (abstract → concrete numbers/names)
- R24: Opinion/attitude (neutral → opinionated)
- R25: Grammar roughness (perfect → natural informal)

**Output**: HUMAN-QUALITY TEXT

---

## Verification Checklist

After final round, verify:

1. ✓ No "delve/tapestry/testament/symphony" vocabulary
2. ✓ No "Furthermore/Moreover/Additionally" connectors
3. ✓ No "shivers ran down/heart pounded" cliches
4. ✓ Dialogue has fragments, interruptions
5. ✓ Sentence length has variation (burstiness)
6. ✓ Paragraphs vary in length
7. ✓ Contains fragments and run-ons
8. ✓ Starts sentences with And/But
9. ✓ Shows emotion through action, not words
10. ✓ Has personality and voice
11. ✓ Specific numbers/names instead of abstract
12. ✓ Opinions/attitude present
