# World Building Prompt — English Only

> Construct a three-dimensional world matrix in English with Western cultural context.

## CRITICAL RULES

1. **OUTPUT MUST BE 100% ENGLISH** — No Chinese characters
2. **Western Cultural Context** — Use Western history, society, geography references
3. **English Place Names** — All locations, regions, factions in English

---

## Input

- Core Seed: {core_seed}
- Character Dynamics: {character_dynamics}
- User Guidance: {user_guidance}

## Task

Build a comprehensive English-language world with three interlocking dimensions.

---

## Three-Dimensional World Matrix

### Dimension 1: PHYSICAL

**Space Structure** (Western geography conventions):
- Regions/zones with English names (New Eden, The Outlands, Core District)
- Social geography (Upper Districts vs Lower Levels, or East/West divide)
- Key locations (5+ with distinct functions): The Citadel, The Archives, The Undercity
- Movement rules: travel methods, restrictions, costs in English context

**Timeline**:
- Current era with English name (The Reconstruction Era, Post-Collapse, The Digital Age)
- Key historical events (3-5 Western-style events: The Great War, The Schism, The Founding)
- Cyclical events (annual festivals, seasonal changes, generational cycles)
- Future pressures: approaching deadlines, threats, events

**Rule Systems**:
- Physical laws (normal physics, modified physics, or magic systems with English names)
- Technology/magic with English terminology
- Social rules with Western legal concepts
- Economic systems with English concepts (credits, standard currency, trade guilds)
- **Identify 2+ rule loopholes** that create conflict opportunities

### Dimension 2: SOCIAL

**Power Structure** (Western political concepts):
- Formal hierarchy: government types (Republic, Federation, Corporation, Monarchy)
- Informal networks: who actually holds power
- Power fault lines: where conflicts brew
- Rising/falling powers: changing dynamics

**Cultural Taboos** (Western social norms):
- Forbidden actions in Western context
- Punishment systems: Western legal concepts (exile, imprisonment, social ostracism)
- Underground violations: who breaks rules secretly
- Taboo triggers: what provokes strongest reactions

**Economic Lifelines**:
- Critical resources in English terms (energy crystals, data banks, water supply)
- Resource control: who holds supply
- Scarcity pressures: where shortages exist
- Opportunity gaps: where new wealth is possible

### Dimension 3: METAPHORICAL

**Visual Symbol System**:
- Recurring motifs with English significance (the Tower = ambition, the River = change)
- Color symbolism: Western associations (red = danger/passion, blue = cold/truth)
- Object symbolism: Western objects with layered meaning
- Architectural symbolism: Western building types (cathedral = faith, tower = power)
- Weather symbolism: Western associations (storm = conflict, fog = uncertainty)

**Environment-Psychology Mapping**:
- Setting-emotion correlations in Western context:
  - Urban decay → moral decline
  - Mountain heights → ambition and isolation
  - Ocean depths → subconscious, hidden truths
- Location-transformation correlation: where characters change
- Environmental pressure: how setting forces behavior

**Civilization Dilemma**:
- Core societal conflict (Western philosophical themes):
  - Individual freedom vs collective security
  - Progress vs tradition
  - Truth vs stability
- Compromise solutions: how society tries to resolve
- Unresolved tension: what remains fractured
- Individual vs collective clash points

## Western Context Examples

**Sci-Fi Setting**:
- Regions: The Core Worlds, The Outer Rim, The Frontier
- History: The Expansion Wars, The AI Rebellion, The Consolidation
- Power: The Federation Council, The Corporate Consortium, The Free Traders
- Taboo: Memory editing (illegal but practiced)
- Symbols: Neural networks = interconnected fate, Stars = infinite possibility

**Fantasy Setting**:
- Regions: The Northern Kingdoms, The Southern Desert, The Eastern Isles
- History: The Demon Wars, The Golden Age, The Sundering
- Power: The High Council, The Guild of Mages, The Knight Orders
- Taboo: Blood magic (prohibited, practiced by outcasts)
- Symbols: The Crown = legitimate power, The Sword = justice through force

**Thriller Setting**:
- Regions: Downtown, The Suburbs, The Industrial Zone
- History: The Corruption Scandal, The Reform Movement, The Crime Wars
- Power: The Mayor's Office, The Police Department, The Syndicate
- Taboo: Snitching (social taboo, legal necessity)
- Symbols: The Badge = authority/corruption dichotomy, The Gun = power
