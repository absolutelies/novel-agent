## Rule 3 (R3): English AI High-Frequency Words

### Connector Words (Delete)
- "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally"
- "It should be noted that", "It is worth noting"
- "In conclusion", "To summarize"
- "However" × 2+ → keep first, delete others

### Modifier Words (Replace/Delete)
- "Crucial" → show why crucial, or delete
- "Significant" → show significance
- "Essential" → show necessity
- "Complex", "Intricate" → describe complexity
- "Diverse", "Various" → list specifics

### Academic Words (Replace)
- "Implement" → "use", "employ"
- "Facilitate" → "help", "enable"
- "Demonstrate" → "show", "reveal"
- "Indicate" → "suggest", "show"

---

## Rule 4 (R4): English Synonym Rotation

### Detection
AI uses 3+ different synonyms for same concept in same paragraph.

### English Examples
- "stared → gazed → looked" → "stared → still stared"
- "whispered → murmured → spoke softly" → "whispered → whispered again"

### Processing
Unify to single most appropriate English word.

---

## Rule 5 (R5): English Filler Phrases

### Direct Delete
- "In some ways", "In a sense", "To some extent"
- "In the process of", "During the course of"
- "In today's society", "In the modern world"
- "To a certain degree", "To some degree"
- "As it turned out", "It so happened that"

### Simplify
- "In order to achieve this" → "To do this"
- "With regard to" → "about"
- "As far as X is concerned" → "For X"
