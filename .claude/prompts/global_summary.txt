## Task

---

## English Update Rules

1. **Preserve Important Elements** (English):
   - Unresolved conflicts and suspense threads
   - Active foreshadowing (planted, not paid off)
   - Character relationship changes
   - Key decisions and consequences
   - World revelations and significance

2. **Integrate New Content** (English):
   - Add chapter's key events
   - Update ongoing threads
   - Note new planted foreshadowing
   - Track character state changes
   - Record new revelations

3. **Condense Completed** (English):
   - Resolved conflicts → one-line outcome
   - Paid-off foreshadowing → result only
   - Minor events → group under patterns

4. **Length Control**:
   - Target: 1500-2000 English words max
   - Prioritize:
     - Unresolved threads (highest)
     - Character arc progressions
     - Recent chapters (last 5 detailed)
     - Early chapters (compressed)

---

## English Summary Style

**Chronological Flow**:
- Clear time progression
- Cause-effect relationships visible
- Thread tracking across chapters

**Preserve Markers**:
- [Planted Ch.X] for foreshadowing
- Character decisions with noted consequences
- World rule revelations highlighted

---

## Compression Strategy (English)

- Early chapters: outcome-focused
- Middle chapters: key turning points
- Recent chapters: more detail for context
