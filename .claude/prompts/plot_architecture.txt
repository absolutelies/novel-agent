## Western Three-Act Structure

### ACT ONE: TRIGGER (Setup)

**Western Opening Conventions**:
- Establish protagonist in status quo
- Show character's flaw/need
- Plant visual symbols
- Introduce stakes subtly

**Elements**:

**Opening Image**: [English description]
- Protagonist's current life
- Visual representation of their flaw
- Symbolic setting

**Anomaly Signs** (3 incidents in English):
- Incident 1: Small deviation
- Incident 2: Escalating pattern
- Incident 3: Direct confrontation

**Threads Introduced** (English terms):
- Main thread: Primary conflict
- Hidden thread: Concealed subplot
- Secondary thread: Supporting storyline

**Inciting Incident** (Western concept):
- Specific trigger event
- Forces protagonist out of comfort zone
- Changes 3+ character relationships

**Wrong Choice** (Western arc element):
- Protagonist's flawed initial response
- Based on character's weakness
- Creates new problems

---

### ACT TWO: CONFRIGNATION (Rising Action)

**Western Rising Action Conventions**:
- Progressive complications
- Tests protagonist's commitment
- Midpoint reversal
- All-is-lost moment

**Elements**:

**Thread Intersection**:
- Main and hidden threads cross
- New complication arises
- Forces strategy change

**External Obstacles** (3 in English):
- Obstacle 1: Physical/external barrier
- Obstacle 2: Increased difficulty
- Obstacle 3: Seeming impossibility

**Internal Defeat**:
- Failure/shame/loss moment
- Confronts character flaw
- Catalyzes transformation need

**False Victory** (Western midpoint):
- Seeming success
- Actually deepens hidden crisis
- False sense of security

**Soul's Dark Night** (Western all-is-lost):
- Maximum external + internal crisis
- Worldview fundamentally challenged
- Truth revelation about hidden thread
- Old beliefs must be abandoned

---

### ACT THREE: RESOLUTION (Climax)

**Western Climax Conventions**:
- Protagonist faces ultimate test
- Must sacrifice something core
- Transformation completes
- New equilibrium

**Elements**:

**Cost Revelation**:
- Solution requires sacrifice
- Trade-off forces final choice
- Protagonist must decide what matters most

**Nested Twists** (3 layers, English):
- Surface: Apparent resolution
- Deeper: Reveals new crisis
- Final: Ultimate choice and consequence

**Resolution Execution**:
- Final choice made
- Sacrifice enacted
- Main thread resolved (or intentionally unresolved)

**Aftermath** (Western ending):
- New equilibrium
- Character transformation complete
- 2+ open threads for potential continuation

## Foreshadowing Strategy (Western Fiction)

**Planted Elements**:
- Physical clues: objects, locations, descriptions
- Dialogue hints: statements with double meaning
- Behavior flags: character reactions with hidden reasons
- Symbol seeding: visual motifs introduced early

**Reinforced Elements**:
- Callback references: earlier clues mentioned again
- Pattern recognition: repeated motifs gaining significance
- Character acknowledgment: protagonist notices anomalies

**Payoff Moments**:
- Twist reveals: hidden meanings exposed
- Symbol culmination: motifs reach peak significance
- Thread resolution: planted clues resolve plot threads
