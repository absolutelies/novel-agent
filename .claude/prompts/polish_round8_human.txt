## R21: Personal Voice Injection

**AI lacks personality. Human writing has voice.**

| AI Lack | Human Addition |
|---------|---------------|
| Neutral tone | Add opinions, attitude |
| Safe statements | Take risks, bold claims |
| Abstract description | Specific, idiosyncratic details |
| Perfect grammar | Natural fragments |
| Formal vocabulary | Casual words, colloquialisms |
| Balanced perspective | Strong positions, preference |

**Example**:
```
AI: "The restaurant was elegant and the service was attentive."
→ Human: "The restaurant tried too hard. The waiter hovered like a guilty parent."

AI: "The situation was difficult for everyone involved."
→ Human: "Nobody won. Nobody even came close."

AI: "He considered the options carefully."
→ Human: "He thought about it. Then thought about it again. Same answer."
```

---

## R22: Imperfection Injection

**AI is too perfect. Humans have rough edges.**

| Technique | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Sentence fragments | "Not happening." / "Done." |
| Run-on sentences | "He kept going and going and nobody stopped him because nobody could." |
| Start with And/But | "And then she left." / "But that wasn't the end." |
| Rhetorical questions | "What was she supposed to do?" |
| Asides/digressions | "—which, looking back, was probably a mistake—" |
| Conversational intrusion | "Look, the thing is..." |
| Opinion intrusion | "He was wrong, obviously." |
| Informal phrasing | "kind of", "sort of", "pretty much" |

---

## R23: Specificity Injection

**AI is abstract. Humans are specific.**

| AI Abstract | Human Specific |
|-------------|----------------|
| "some time" | "three hours", "half a morning" |
| "a few people" | "maybe five", "the three of them" |
| "the room" | "the kitchen", "the small office" |
| "a noise" | "a click", "a scrape" |
| "something" | Name the thing |
| "somewhere" | Name the place |
| "a feeling" | Name the feeling or show action |
| "various items" | List actual items |

---

## R24: Opinion/Attitude Injection

**AI is neutral. Humans have opinions.**

| AI Neutral | Human Opinionated |
|------------|-------------------|
| "It was a difficult decision." | "It was the wrong decision, but it was hers to make." |
| "The plan had some issues." | "The plan was garbage. But it was all they had." |
| "He was an interesting character." | "He was exhausting—brilliant, but exhausting." |
| "The weather was unpleasant." | "The weather was crap. Typical." |

---

## R25: Final Grammar Roughness

**Small imperfections that feel human**:

| Perfect AI | Rough Human |
|------------|-------------|
| "He did not go." | "He didn't go." |
| "She could not believe." | "She couldn't believe it." |
| "It was not until..." | "Not until..." |
| "There were many people." | "Lot of people." |
| Perfect comma usage | Strategic comma omission |
| No double negatives | "It wasn't nothing." (dialogue only) |
