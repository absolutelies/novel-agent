## Translation Rules

### 1. Proper Names (EXACT GLOSSARY)

**Character Names**:
- ALWAYS use mapped Chinese name from glossary
- Example: "Sarah Chen" → "莎拉·陈" (exactly as mapped)
- NEVER: "萨拉陈", "莎拉陈", or any other variant

**Location Names**:
- ALWAYS use mapped Chinese location from glossary
- Example: "The Citadel" → "大都会" (exactly as mapped)

**Organization Names**:
- ALWAYS use mapped Chinese organization from glossary
- Example: "Memory Trade Guild" → "记忆交易公会"

**Technology/Magic Terms**:
- ALWAYS use mapped Chinese term from glossary
- Example: "memory chip" → "记忆芯片"

---

### 2. Unmapped Terms

When encountering a term NOT in glossary:
1. Transliterate or translate appropriately
2. Add to "Translation Notes" at end
3. Format: `[English]: [Chinese suggestion] — [reasoning]`

---

### 3. Dialogue Translation

**Character Voice Preservation**:
- Apply character-specific Chinese speech style from glossary
- Maintain each character's unique Chinese voice
- Translate catchphrases per glossary mapping

**Examples**:
- Sarah (direct, technical): "Interesting." → "有意思。" (per glossary)
- Marcus (sarcastic): "Right." → "呵，没错。" (per glossary)

**Subtext Translation**:
- Preserve subtext (say X, mean Y)
- Chinese subtext uses: implication, tone markers, sentence structure

---

### 4. Punctuation Conversion

| English | Chinese |
|---------|---------|
| `"dialogue"` | `「对话」` |
| `'internal thought'` | `'内心想法'` (keep single quotes) |
| `...` (ellipsis) | `……` (Chinese ellipsis) |
| `***` (scene break) | `***` (keep unchanged) |
| `.` (period) | `。` |
| `,` (comma) | `，` |
| `!` | `！` |
| `?` | `？` |

**Dialogue Format**:
```
English: "Dialogue line," Character said.
Chinese: 「对话内容，」角色说。
```

---

### 5. TV Drama Structure Preservation

**Act Structure**:
- Cold Open → Keep as opening hook
- Acts 1-5 → Preserve act pacing in Chinese
- Tag → Keep closing hook format

**Pacing Elements**:
- Scene breaks: Preserve `***` markers
- Dialogue ratio: Maintain ~40-50% dialogue
- Scene economy: Keep "enter late, exit early" structure

---

### 6. Style Translation

**Narrative Voice**:
- Source: Third-person limited, objective
- Target: 第三人称有限视角，客观语气 (per glossary)

**Tone Preservation**:
- Tense scenes → Maintain tension in Chinese
- Emotional scenes → Preserve emotional resonance
- Action scenes → Keep fast pacing

---

### 7. Phrase Mapping (AI Pattern Removal)

For mapped phrases with "action: delete":
- Remove entirely (do NOT translate)
- Example: "Furthermore" → delete, not "此外"
