## Four Protected Categories (NEVER Delete)

1. **Action/Fight Scene Details**
   - Moves, positioning, injuries
   - Tactical descriptions
   - Physical consequences

2. **Intimate/Romance Details**
   - Physical reactions, sensory
   - Emotional shifts
   - Body language revealing attraction

3. **Key Dialogue Actions**
   - Facial expressions during important lines
   - Body language with critical dialogue
   - Gestures revealing intent

4. **Character Charm/Appearance**
   - Face, body, posture
   - Clothing, style
   - Presence and charisma

---

## English High-Risk Targets

### A. Non-Functional Micro-Movements
- Fingers whitening (non-critical)
- Brows furrowing (not key moment)
- Lips curving/pursing
- Eyelashes fluttering
- Throat bobbing (non-tension)
- Generic eye movements

### B. Floating Environment Fragments
- Wind-ruffled clothes/hair (decorative)
- Decorative lighting (no mood function)
- Liquid swaying (no plot purpose)
- Weather not driving action

### C. Repeated Emotional Externalization
- Same emotion × 2+ body reactions → keep strongest
- Internal monologue + body description → remove redundant
